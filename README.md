## 🏥 About Sanjeeva Multispeciality Hospital

Sanjeeva Hospital originally started as **Sanjeeva Clinic**, a humble single-room OPD setup dedicated to outpatient care.

Driven by ethical healthcare and the trust of the local community, the hospital expanded its facilities and inpatient capacity over the years.

Today, Sanjeeva Multispeciality Hospital has developed into a **50-bed multispecialty healthcare center**, providing modern diagnostics, pediatric care, maternity services, surgical care, emergency support, and other essential medical services.

Our goal is simple:

> **Health Care with Ethics**

# 🏥 Our Evolution

## Our Glorious Healthcare Journey

Providing trusted healthcare services across the Nandyala region since inception.

### Inception — Sanjeeva Clinic

Started as a small, single-room OPD section dedicated to outpatient services.

Within the first year, the facility expanded to approximately **10 beds**, and within two years it developed into a **20-bed hospital setup**.

### 2016 — Laboratory & Diagnostics

Established clinical laboratory and diagnostic services to provide reliable medical testing for patients and the surrounding community.

### 2018 — Operation Theatre Expansion

Introduced sterilized operation theatre facilities for minor, general, and major surgical procedures.

### 2020 — CPAP & HFNC Integration

Introduced advanced pediatric respiratory support systems, including:

- CPAP
- High-Flow Nasal Cannula (HFNC)
- Pediatric critical-care support

### 2022 — Modernized Surgical & Diagnostic Facilities

Expanded modern medical infrastructure with:

- Laparoscopic surgical systems
- Digital X-Ray
- Computerized laboratory services
- Advanced diagnostic facilities

### 2024 — Advanced Ultrasound Services

Integrated high-resolution ultrasound imaging for prenatal and internal-organ diagnostic requirements.

### 2026 — Premium 50-Bed Multispecialty Center

Developed into a modern **50-bed multispecialty healthcare center** providing specialized services in:

- Pediatrics
- Neonatal care
- Gynecology & Obstetrics
- General Surgery
- Laparoscopic Surgery
- ENT
- Emergency & Trauma Care
- Diagnostics
